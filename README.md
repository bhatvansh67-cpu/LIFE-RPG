# 🎮 LIFE-RPG

> Turn your real life into a game.

LIFE-RPG is a personal productivity RPG that turns everyday goals and tasks into **quests, XP, levels, achievements, streaks, and virtual rewards**.

The goal isn't just to make productivity look like a game — it's to create a system that makes you **commit to your goals and actually follow through**.

---

## ✨ Features

### ⚔️ Quest System
Create and complete real-life quests.

- Custom quest titles
- Difficulty levels
- XP rewards
- Coin rewards
- Quest categories
- Completion tracking
- Daily objectives

### 📈 XP & Leveling

Complete quests to gain XP and level up your character.
